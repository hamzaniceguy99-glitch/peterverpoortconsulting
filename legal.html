Terms of service

What we provide

Peter Verpoort provides business support services and head office support functions provided by Peter Verpoort Consulting, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 0768.598.997, with an establishment at Lier. IMPORTANT — NO SUPPLY OF PERSONNEL: Article 31 of the Law of 24 July 1987 PROHIBITS, as a matter of principle, the making available of personnel to a third party who exercises over them any part of the authority belonging to an employer. Temporary agency work is lawful only where carried out by an agency holding the recognition issued by the competent Region. THE COMPANY DOES NOT MAKE PERSONNEL AVAILABLE TO CLIENTS, does not operate as a temporary employment agency, and does not place candidates in employment for a fee, holding no such regional recognition. Engagements are accordingly structured around defined deliverables performed under the company's own direction and organisation, and a client requiring workers under its own authority is referred to a licensed agency. Where an arrangement would in substance amount to the prohibited making available of personnel, the company declines it: the consequences of that prohibition include the joint and several liability of the user for social security contributions and remuneration, and the deemed existence of an employment contract, so the exposure is the client's as well as the company's. INTERIM MANAGEMENT: where the company performs an interim management assignment, it does so as an independent undertaking on a defined mandate; it is not an employee of the client, does not take a position in the client's hierarchy as an employee would, and retains the organisation of its own working time and methods. SCOPE AND RESERVED ACTIVITIES: the company does not keep books, prepare annual accounts or provide tax advice, which are reserved in Belgium to professionals registered with the Institute for Tax Advisors and Accountants; it does not carry out statutory audit, reserved to registered auditors; it does not provide legal advice or representation, reserved to the Bar; and it does not provide investment advice, financial planning or insurance mediation, which require authorisation from or registration with the FSMA. Such matters are referred to appropriately qualified professionals rather than addressed in the margins of a support engagement. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA: information received about a client's business is treated as confidential; where the company processes personal data on a client's behalf it acts as a processor within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation and only under a written processing agreement complying with its Article 28, the client remaining the controller. DECISIONS: recommendations are advisory and the decision, its implementation and the conduct of the client's business remain the client's own. NO COMMERCIAL, FINANCIAL OR OPERATIONAL RESULT IS GUARANTEED. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
